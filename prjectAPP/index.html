Foundation

Three Core Pillars

Vision 2030 is built on three ambitions that guide every initiative and reform across the Kingdom.

01

Vibrant Society

Strengthening Islamic values, national identity, social responsibility, and quality of life through cultural enrichment and heritage preservation.

Culture & Heritage
02

Thriving Economy

Diversifying income sources, creating job opportunities, and growing the private sector to reduce dependence on oil revenue.

Economic Growth
03

Ambitious Nation

Building a responsible government, effective institutions, and a transparent environment that encourages investment and entrepreneurship.

Governance & Reform
